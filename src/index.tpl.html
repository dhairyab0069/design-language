Design language

Murmur

The one quiet, living style behind every page on this site: alive by default, hushed in one press, honest and plain. Everything below is read from the pages themselves, and most of it you can hear and touch.

Read the principles Try the twelve themes
  • 12 themes, each designed in light and dark
  • 10 named sound cues in 6 palettes
  • 8 transitions, one metaphor per page
  • 1 shared theme setting for every page

Try it now: the header has Light, Dark and Auto, then Sound, Motion, Effects and the moon that quiets everything. Sound starts after your first click or key press, because browsers require it.
